# Product

**Who:** small teams whose members spend money for work.
**Problem:** members need their expenses approved and repaid; approvers need to see what their team submitted.

## Core flows

1. A member records an expense as a draft.
2. The member submits the draft for approval.
3. An approver of the member's team approves or rejects it. *(planned)*

## Out of scope

Payments, receipts, currencies other than one, editing after submission.
