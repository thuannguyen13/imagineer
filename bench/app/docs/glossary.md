# Glossary

- **Expense:** money a member spent for work, in whole cents.
- **Draft:** an expense its owner has not submitted yet; only the owner and admins see it.
- **Submitted:** sent for approval; approvers of the owner's team can see it.
- **Decision:** approving or rejecting a submitted expense (planned).
- **Member, approver, admin:** the roles; see `docs/permissions.md`.
- **Team:** the group an expense belongs to; it is the owner's team when the expense is created.
